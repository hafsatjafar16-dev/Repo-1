# Pricing & Packages — Draft (H1)

Working draft to refine together. Figures below are general industry
ballparks for independent EU cosmetics regulatory consultants, not a
benchmarking study — treat them as a starting anchor to sanity-check and
adjust for positioning, not as fact. Your background — 5 years' regulatory
experience, Global Regulatory Specialist at Reckitt (Veet, Clearasil) and
current Regional Regulatory Lead at ChemView (Neutrogena) — is a genuinely
strong credential for client-facing positioning, and supports pricing toward
the upper end of these ranges, especially for RP services and
complex/novel-ingredient formulas. Worth stating explicitly in your own
marketing/proposals, not just implying it.

## 1. Pick a pricing model per service type, not one model for everything

| Model | Best for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Hourly** | Ad hoc advisory, Q&A retainer top-ups, scope-uncertain work | Fair when scope can't be pinned down in advance |
| **Fixed fee per deliverable** | PIF compilation, label review, CPNP notification, claims review | Client wants price certainty; you control margin once you know your average hours/deliverable |
| **Monthly retainer (hours bucket)** | Ongoing advisory for an existing brand, "on-call" access | Predictable revenue for you, predictable cost for client; sell in tiers (e.g., 5h / 10h / 20h per month) |
| **Per-SKU annual fee** | Acting as Responsible Person (RP-as-a-service) | RP is an ongoing legal obligation tied to the product being on-market, not a one-off project — price it as recurring |

## 2. Draft rate card (starting point — adjust to your market)

- **Hourly advisory rate:** €120–€200/hr is a reasonable starting band for a
  former big-company RA specialist going independent; many established
  independent consultants in this space land €150–€300/hr, especially for
  novel-ingredient, borderline-product-classification, or crisis/recall work.
- **PIF compilation (per SKU):** €800–€2,500, scaling with formula complexity,
  number of claims to substantiate, and whether stability/challenge test data
  already exists or needs to be commissioned.
- **CPSR Part A drafting:** often bundled into the PIF fee above; if quoted
  separately, €400–€1,200 per SKU.
- **Safety Assessor sign-off (Part B):** typically a pass-through cost if you
  subcontract a qualified assessor — quote it as a line item, not absorbed
  into your fee, unless you are the qualified assessor yourself.
- **Label review & compliance check:** €150–€400 per SKU/variant.
- **Claims substantiation review:** €200–€600 per claim set, more for
  higher-risk claims (efficacy, "free-from," hypoallergenic).
- **CPNP notification (submission only, data already prepared):** €100–€300
  per product/variant.
- **RP-as-a-service (annual, per SKU):** €500–€1,500/year, often with a setup
  fee (€200–€500) for onboarding a new product line. Multi-market (EU + UK +
  CH) should be priced as separate RP engagements, not a discount bundle,
  since each is a distinct legal obligation.
- **Monthly retainer (advisory hours):** price the hours at a modest discount
  to your standalone hourly rate (e.g., 10–15% off) in exchange for revenue
  predictability — e.g., 10h/month retainer at €135/hr effective vs. €150/hr
  ad hoc.

## 3. Example packages (bundle for clarity, price the bundle not just the sum)

**"Market Entry Essentials"** (single SKU, EU launch)
PIF compilation + CPSR Part A + label review + CPNP notification support.
Target price: bundle the line items above with a modest (~10%) bundling
discount vs. buying separately, and a clear inclusions/exclusions list (e.g.,
Safety Assessor fee and any lab testing quoted separately as pass-through).

**"Full Compliance Package"** (single SKU, multi-market EU + UK)
Everything in Market Entry Essentials + UK SCPN notification + UK RP
coordination + claims substantiation review. Price as Market Entry Essentials
+ a defined UK add-on, so the marginal cost of each extra market is visible to
the client (and to you, when scoping).

**"Ongoing Advisory Retainer"**
Monthly hours bucket (e.g., 5h/10h/20h) for ad hoc questions, formula/label
change reviews, Annex-update monitoring, claims sanity checks. This is the
natural home for "recurring compliance check" work flagged in the market-entry
checklist — sell it explicitly rather than letting it become unpaid follow-up.

## 4. Practical notes

- Quote **PIF/CPSR/label work as fixed fee once you know your average hours
  per SKU** (see `consulting-hours-capacity.md`) — clients strongly prefer
  price certainty for defined deliverables, and fixed fee protects your
  margin once you're fast at the templated parts.
- Keep **RP-as-a-service separate from project fees** — it's a different risk
  profile (ongoing legal liability, not a one-off task) and should never be
  a "free add-on" to win a project.
- Always separate **pass-through costs** (Safety Assessor fees if
  subcontracted, lab testing, translation) from your own fee — quote them as
  disbursements, not absorbed margin.
- Revisit rates annually; a common failure mode for new independent
  consultants is underpricing early engagements and then struggling to raise
  rates with the same client later — better to price close to target from the
  start and offer a founding-client discount explicitly (with an end date)
  than to underprice indefinitely.
